   - func_range in scripts/odin_usb_deep.py cuts at the FIRST ret within
     0x4000, while function_bounds in scripts/abl_string_xref.py scans back
     to a prologue and forward to the first ret from the prologue. Interior
     labels of one function therefore get different windows in the two
     scripts. Observed case: bl targets 0x040b8c/0x040c50/0x040b7c/0x040b9c/
     0x040c98 all resolve via function_bounds to the SAME bounds

     not five independent VERIFY functions. Stage1 counts them separately;
